# System Boundaries
This outlines the scope and boundaries of the project.

## Scope and scale
- The software will be used by 8-12 crew members concurrently. 
- The most often that the software will be used is around every month. 
- The software will only be used in Australia. 
- It will be used during concerts and events.

## Inclusions
All essential features listed in the functional requirements will be implemented.

## Exclusions
The software will not support the live synchronised editing of run sheets seen in some SaaS applications such as Google Docs.

## Time constraints
The project must be completed in 11 weeks (during Term 2), with an additional 2-4 weeks (during Term 1 and the school holidays) available. Balancing the project with other subjects and commitments will also be tricky.

## Other constraints
I do not have experience in backend development, yet a backend will serve as a core part of my project.
