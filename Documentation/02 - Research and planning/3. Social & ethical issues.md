# Social and ethical issues
This page outlines social/ethical issues that are relevant to the project.

## Maintaining transparency with stakeholders/clients
Open communication with stakeholders should be transparent in order to act in an ethical manner. Information about any progress, challenges, changes, executive decisions and leadership structures (if applicable) should be shared to keep stakeholders adequately informed and build trust.

In this project, communication between developers and clients will be as transparent as possible, allowing them to holistically understand and engage with the project. This allows them to provide accurate feedback, but also understand why their feedback may or may not have been implemented into the software solution.

Without transparent communication, trust between developers and the stakeholders (teachers and senior crew members) erodes. If developers are not transparent, it can lead to misunderstandings and cause stakeholders to question the honesty and credibility of the developer. This would disrupt the cycle of feedback during the development of this system, causing this project to stray away from its WAgile approach. This project would lack feedback from a diverse audience, leading to a poorer software solution. More generally, a lack of trust makes a company more "likely [to] lose employees, customers, and the benefit of the doubt of other critical stakeholders".[^1]
