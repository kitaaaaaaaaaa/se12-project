# Communication with clients
The project will have multiple clients providing feedback on the software, since no individual client has the availability for regular communication.

These two clients are of varying ages and backgrounds, and their feedback will provide great insight into the usability of the software for different users. By taking their feedback on board, it improves the final product by allowing it to cater towards a wide audience.

| Client | Communication Channel |
| - | - |
| Teachers in AV Crew | Email/in person meetings <br> Agreed on response within 1 week |
| Senior crew members | Chatting apps/email/in person <br> Agreed on response within 1 week |
