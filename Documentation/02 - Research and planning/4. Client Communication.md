# Communication with clients
The project will have multiple clients providing feedback on the software, since no individual client has the availability for regular communication.

These two clients are of varying ages and backgrounds, and their feedback will provide great insight into the usability of the software for different users. By taking their feedback on board, it improves the final product by allowing it to cater towards a wide audience.

| Client | Communication Channel |
| - | - |
| A teacher in AV Crew | Email/in person meetings <br> Agreed on response within 1 week |
| Senior crew members | Chatting apps/email/in person <br> Agreed on response within 1 week |

## Communication strategies
To maintain appropriate communication with stakeholders/clients, the following strategies will be employed.

### Empowering clients
Clients will be empowered by being a key part of the decision making and development of the project. Open and transparent communication will be maintained so that they can be adequately informed. Frequent communication gives the clients more opportunity to collaborate and share their vision.

In person meetings will be the main way of informing and empowering clients, since emails can be easily missed.

### Enabling feedback
Any communication with clients will be done with an open and inclusive attitude, creating a supportive environment for them to provide feedback. Through in person meetings, clients find it easier to provide feedback, leading to a better product.

Any incorporation of feedback will be shown to clients to demonstrate the acceptance of such feedback, inviting clients to provide further feedback.

### Negotiating successfully
Sometimes, feedback provided by clients will conflict with technical, temporal or project limitations and negotiation must be used. To have successful and respectful negotiation throughout the project involves avoiding dismissive attitudes and taking time to properly consider user feedback. If feedback cannot be incorporated, it will be explained to clients why it was not taken on board, and a compromise will be met.

This will strengthen the relationship with clients and empower clients by involving them with the technical aspects of the project.
